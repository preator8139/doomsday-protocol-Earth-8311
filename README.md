## Campaign Idea

Unleash Your Edge is a youth-focused campaign encouraging students and young adults to challenge their limits, embrace new experiences and turn everyday moments into opportunities to perform, create and achieve.

## Target Audience

Students and young adults aged 18–30 in India who want more energy, excitement and motivation to take on challenges in college, sports, gaming, creativity and everyday life.

## Customer Benefits

- **Push Your Limits** – “I want the energy to take on my next challenge.”
- **Stay in the Moment** – “I don't want to slow down when things get exciting.”
- **Make It Happen** – “Give me the push to turn an idea into action.”
